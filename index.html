WORKOUT LOGGING

Log every set. See what you lifted.

Follow a ready-made program or build your own. Every exercise has an animated demo and form cues, a rest timer runs between sets, and when you finish you see your time, sets and total weight lifted.

  • Animated demos and "how to do it" cues for every move, with realistic male and female demos for the most common lifts being added every day
  • Personal bests flagged as you train, and a finish screen that compares your total lift to everyday things like animals, vehicles and logs

PROGRESS TRACKING

Log your weigh-ins. Watch the trend.

Record weight and measurements, add progress photos and do a one-minute weekly check-in. Your health score and weekly momentum score show how the week is really going, even on a rotating roster.

MEAL PLANNING

A week of meals and the shopping list, done.

Set your calories and preferences, and RespondFit builds a week of healthy meals sized to your target, with a shopping list. Log what you eat in the food diary, and use the macro calculator to work out your numbers.

  • Vegetarian, dairy-free, gluten-free and quick options

BEEP TEST

Run the beep test with your phone.

Fitness assessment coming up? The built-in 20 m multistage beep test plays the beeps, shows your level and shuttle, and gives your spotter a "missed the line" button. Your results are saved, and running programs build you up to your goal.

  • Setup checklist, sound test and a 10-second countdown

Questions

Who is RespondFit for?

Firefighters, police, paramedics, SES and rescue volunteers, defence personnel, and anyone preparing to join a service. It's built around shift work, fitness assessments and the physical demands of the job.

Does it work if my roster changes every week?

Yes. You set weekly targets rather than fixed days, so you can train whenever your shifts allow. Your weekly score resets every Monday.

Can it help me prepare for a fitness test?

Yes. Pick your service to get a test set for police, fire, ambulance, rescue or defence, log your results, set targets and a test date, and see how ready you are. The beep test and running programs are built in too. The starting targets are typical benchmarks, not an official standard, so check your own service's current requirements.

What is Fit Bot?

Fit Bot is an AI training buddy on your Home screen. Ask it about workouts, running, recovery or nutrition and it answers using your profile, roster and weekly progress. It gives general guidance only, not medical advice.

Does it connect to Apple Health, Samsung Health or Garmin?

You can import a file exported from those apps to bring in steps, sleep, weight and workouts from the last 90 days. It is a one-off import, not a live sync.

Is the Wellbeing section private?

Your daily check-ins stay on your device. The page lists support lines, but it is not medical advice or a replacement for professional support.

Are my progress photos private?

Progress photos are stored on your device. If you're connected to a coach, your coach can see the photos you add. Community posts are visible to other RespondFit members.

Is this medical advice?

No. RespondFit gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. Stop if anything hurts, and check with a doctor first if you have an injury or health condition.
